I'm a second-year design student who stumbled into web design and never looked back. What started as curiosity — a blank HTML file and a flickering idea — has grown into a genuine passion for crafting digital experiences that feel alive.

About

Still a student, fully.

I'm still learning, and I love that. Right now I'm deep in the world of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, building the foundation that will carry everything I dream of making.

Every project is a new problem to solve, a new chance to close the gap between how something looks and how it feels to use.

I believe the best designers are the ones who never stop being students — and I'm leaning into that fully. I'm not just here to graduate. I'm here to grow into someone who builds things that matter.

The journey is just beginning, and I'm moving with intention, one project at a time.
